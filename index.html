The Vault

Progress saves to this device automatically, and to the host app when one is available. Vault codes are the portable copy — MB75 for Malik, JM75 for Janel. Copy yours to move a ledger to another device, or paste either code here to restore it.

The Rules

  • One workout a day, 20–30 minutes.
  • Balanced meals with protein and vegetables. No fast food, no sugar.
  • Drink 1.5–2 liters of water daily.
  • Read 10 pages of any book.
  • Sleep at least 7 hours each night.
  • Milestone photos on days 1, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 75.

How It Works

  • Tap a day to open it, then tap a habit to check it off. Use ‹ and › to step a day at a time.
  • Part-done days carry a bar along the bottom showing how far you got.
  • A filled tile is a perfect day — all five habits, or all six on a photo day.
  • Today is ringed. Days after today are dimmed until you reach them.
  • A dot in the corner marks a milestone photo day. The photo box stays locked on every other day and doesn't count against you.
  • Streak counts perfect days back from today. Today stays open until you complete it, so it only breaks once a finished day is missed.
  • Malik and Janel are separate ledgers — own start date, own progress, own colours. Switch with the tabs at the top.
  • Progress saves by itself on this device as you tap. To carry a ledger to another phone, copy its vault code and paste it there.
